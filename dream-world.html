The big dream

The Dream World

Close your eyes and imagine a world full of positive people. A world where everyone believes that nothing is impossible, and where people come together like one team. This is the world 3rd Human is working towards.

Imagine a world where…

Ten things you would see

Nothing is impossible

Everyone believes in their dreams and in yours.

Trust comes first

Faith and trust matter more than showing off.

One big team

People cheer for each other and help each other win.

Your dream is your name

People know you by your dream, not by your title or your money.

Questions matter most

We are judged by our questions, not only by our answers.

Learning is a joy

Study is a tool we love to use, not a pressure.

Money is a helper

Long-term thinking

Saying 'no' is okay

The heart of the dream

We, not Me

Today the world often thinks about "Me": my marks, my money, my success. The Dream World thinks about "We".

It is a place of learners and seekers. People support each other for a big cause, with no profit motive, and the benefits go to everyone. Big talks and friendly debates are part of every day. Self-help activities are part of every day too. Faith comes first, right after breathing.

"Judge a man by his questions rather than by his answers."Voltaire, as quoted in the Dream World
How do we get there?

Five things everyone needs

Everything is possible if we have these five.

1

The right mindset

To keep working and walking on the path of our dreams.

2

The right picture of me

To know who I am today, and what my goals are.

3

The right resources

The tools, books and skills to reach those goals.

4

The right people around me

Friends and family who support me in a fair and positive way.

5

The right actions

Small, real steps, taken every day.
