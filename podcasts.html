Big ideas from the talks

Try these at home

Small changes that make home the safest place on earth.

From control to connection

Guide your child instead of controlling them. Fear only creates more fear.

Dining table talks

Sit together with phones away. Everyone shares one thing going well and one thing that isn't.

Ask "what stops you?"

When a child is scared of the stage or a test, ask what their inner voice is saying, instead of pushing.

Stop comparing

Know your child's own interests and dreams. Comparison takes away their identity.

The three-jar money game

Split pocket money into three jars: spend now, save for soon, and keep for the future.

No blame game

Parents and teachers are one team for the child, not opponents.

Keep the roots

Share family stories and culture, so children know where they come from.
