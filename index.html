Welcome to 3rd Human

Where happiness is the currency.

3rd Human is a dream for a new kind of world. In this world, people know themselves, think for themselves, speak with kindness and live for each other.

We are building it step by step, with books, apps, talks and a brand new language called Onee.

The story of us

The three humans

Every one of us carries all three. The 3rd Human is the one we can choose to grow.

1

The 1st Human learned to survive

Long ago, humans lived like animals. Step by step they learned everything: fire, tools, homes and farming. They built things so they could stay alive.

2

The 2nd Human learned to compete

This is the world most of us live in today. It runs on money and comparison: who has more, who is ahead, who lives the richest life. It is busy and full of progress, but often not happy.

3

The 3rd Human learns to love

The 3rd Human keeps learning about themselves, lives for all humans, and works on projects that build humanity. Here money is not the currency. Happiness is.

What we want to do

Our four big goals

3rd Human has four clear goals. Each goal has its own books and tools.

♥Goal: Feel

Help every child understand their feelings

Feelings are messengers, not problems. When children can name a feeling, they can handle it.

Tools: EmoNav, Emotional Stethoscope, Building Tomorrow

?Goal: Think

Teach people how to think, not what to think

Ask good questions. Look from many sides. Change your mind when you learn something new.

Tools: Conspread, Belief Ledger, Through the Looking Glass

OGoal: Speak

Give the world a kinder language

Onee is a new language with no shame, no blame and respect for every view. Its first word means "We are One".

Tools: Onee Language

✿Goal: Grow together

Bring families and schools closer

Home and school should be the safest places to share a feeling or a mistake. Grown-ups guide; they don't control.

Tools: Podcasts, Meeting Myself, Sessions

Who is a 3rd Human?

Six habits of a 3rd Human

You don't need to be perfect. You just need to practise these habits a little every day.

A

Self-aware

I notice what I feel and what I think. I can name my feelings instead of hiding them.

R

Responsible

I own my choices and my words. When I make a mistake, I fix it and learn. No blame game.

F

Self-focused

I grow at my own speed. I compare myself with who I was yesterday, not with others.

S

Strength-based

I know what I am good at and I build on it. Everyone has a superpower, and so do I.

Q

A free thinker

I ask questions. My first thought is not always true, so I check it.

W

We before Me

I think about everyone, not just me. A better me makes a better we.

How it works

The 3rd Human path

Try these five steps the next time something big happens.

  1. Stop and noticeTake one slow breath. What am I feeling? Where do I feel it in my body?
  2. Name itFind the right word. "I feel left out" helps more than "I feel bad".
  3. Check my thoughtIs my first thought true? Is there another way to see this?
  4. Choose my actionWhat would the person I want to become do now?
  5. Use my strengthWhich of my superpowers can help me here?
