EmoNav

Hear yourself first. EmoNav is like a mirror for your words. You speak, you hear how you sound, and then you decide what you really want to say.

Open EmoNav
Two doors

Pick the one that fits right now

1

I'm in it right now

Something just happened and the feeling is big. Let it out, hear yourself, and find a calmer way to see it.

2

I'm preparing for something

A test talk, an interview, saying sorry, a hard chat. Practise it out loud and see how it might land on the listener.

How it works

Six simple steps

  1. Speak freelyTap the button and talk, or type. No one is judging here. Tell EmoNav who will hear your words.
  2. Hear yourselfListen back to how you sounded.
  3. See how it landsEmoNav shows how a fair listener might feel, and the same words without the angry or upset charge.
  4. DecideSay it again, or keep it. Trying again is part of the practice.
  5. Name the feelingEmoNav suggests a feeling and a next step. It is a guess, not a verdict, so you can choose your own word.
  6. Save to your journalOver time, see what you feel most and what keeps coming back.
Safe by design

Your moments stay with you

  • Your journal is saved only on your own device, in your browser.
  • Your voice is checked on your device too.
  • EmoNav is a mirror, not a counsellor.
  • If someone is in distress, EmoNav shows free Indian helplines: Tele-MANAS 14416, KIRAN 1800-599-0019 and Childline 1098.
