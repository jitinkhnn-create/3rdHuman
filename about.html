Hello, I'm Jitin

I am Jitin Khanna: an educator, life coach and author. I spent more than 15 years working with numbers in big companies. There I learned that numbers matter, but people matter more.

My vision

A world full of happy, creative people

I want to fill the world with happy and creative people, each with an account in the Bank of Happiness.

My mission is to help children and grown-ups understand themselves, look at daily life in new ways, and get ready for a world shaped by AI, a world where human values matter more than things.

"Better we, because of better me."Jitin Khanna

Invite me

Sessions for children, grown-ups and schools

I run interactive sessions in schools, libraries and NGOs. Every session uses simple, step-by-step ideas.

For children

  • Handling stress and worry
  • Making your own emergency kit for hard days
  • Building your own Emotional Stethoscope
  • Staying calm, trusting yourself, staying focused
  • Knowing who you are and who you want to become

For grown-ups

  • Seeing life in a new way
  • Creative ways to find happiness and keep it
  • Looking again at simple words like success and failure
  • Getting ready for life's emergencies
  • Naming feelings and calming them

For schools and NGOs

  • The Building Tomorrow programme
  • Teacher guidance for open, fear-free classrooms
  • Parent sessions on guiding, not controlling
