A thinking notebook

Belief Ledger

A ledger is a notebook for careful records. The Belief Ledger is a notebook for your beliefs. You write what you believe, what would change your mind, and the day you learned something new.

How to keep one

Three steps, one honest mind

  1. Write the beliefSomething you think is true. For example: "I am bad at maths."
  2. Write what would change your mindDecide this before you check. For example: "If I score well on two tests in a row."
  3. Write the date it changedWhen the proof arrives, update the belief or cross it out, and write the date.

Why it matters

Changing your mind is a strength

Some beliefs help us and some hold us back. A 3rd Human is brave enough to check. Changing your mind when you learn something new is not losing. It is growing.

The Belief Ledger starts as a printable workbook. An app version will follow.
