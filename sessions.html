Sometimes reading is not enough, and you need someone to think it through with. A session is a calm, private conversation with Jitin Khanna about what you are going through and what you want for yourself.

What to expect

No judgement. No labels.

  • You talk about what is really going on, in your own words.
  • Together, you look at what sits underneath it: the feeling, the belief, the pattern.
  • You leave with one or two simple practices to try in your daily life.
  • What you share stays between you and Jitin.
