A new language

Onee

Onee is a new language created by Jitin Khanna for a better world. Its words are built for respect, clear talk, feelings and togetherness. In Onee there is no right or wrong, no shame, and every view matters.

Ekay. We are One.

Start here

The three roots of Onee

A root is a small seed word. Many other words grow from it. Onee grows from three seeds.

Ekaysay "eh-kay"

The One

Unity, oneness, being. Ekay is the self and everything that connects us all.

Deksay "dehk"

To see, to understand

Awareness, clarity, perspective. Dek is the act of seeing clearly.

Navsay "naav"

New, now, forward

Growth, creation, change. Nav is the flow of becoming something new.

Ekay is our foundation. Dek is our awareness. Nav is our becoming.

Build your own words

Roots join together like building blocks

Put roots side by side and their meanings join too.

Nav+Dek=Navdek

A new understanding, a fresh way of seeing. "Her words gave me Navdek."

Dek+Ekay=Dekay

Seeing yourself truly. "Take time to Dekay before you speak."

Ekay+Nav=Ekaynav

Growing together as one. "Our team moved forward in Ekaynav."

Ekay+yu=Ekayu

Adding -yu turns an idea into an action. Ekayu means doing something together.

Talk about feelings

Feeling words in Onee

In Onee, a feeling is something passing through you. It is not who you are.

Elunsay "eh-lun"calm and clear
Aaravaassay "aa-ra-vaas"peaceful
Solmirsay "sol-meer"hopeful
Orruvaassay "or-roo-vaas"unsure or scared
Kalensay "ka-len"confused, mixed up
Miraasay "mi-raa"caring about how someone else feels
Virrasay "vi-raa"brave, full of courage
Navaasay "na-vaa"healing, getting better

How to say a feeling: Mee + feeling + Kaas. For example, "Mee Kalen kaas." means "Confusion is passing through me right now." It does not say "I am confused." You are not your feeling.

A kinder way to talk

English and Onee, side by side

Onee is built to keep a talk open, even when people disagree.

In English"You are wrong."

This can feel like an attack. People get defensive and stop listening.

In Onee"Mee dek vidaas."

"My view wants to explore this more." The talk stays open and friendly.

Turning a fight into a talk, in four steps

  1. Mee dek…"My view right now is…" Own your view.
  2. Mee orruvaas kaas…"I feel unsure or scared…" Share the feeling.
  3. Mee tavsurr firr…"I need an honest talk…" Say what you need.
  4. Vida ekay?"Shall we explore this together?" Invite the other person in.

The rules of Onee

Six promises inside the language

No right or wrong

We explore ideas instead of judging them.

No shame

A weakness is just a stage, not who you are.

Every view matters

Each view is true for the person who holds it.

Truth with kindness

Speak the truth without hurting anyone's dignity.

Growth over labels

We talk about who we are becoming, not fixed labels.

We before Me

Togetherness comes before ego.
