Conspread

Think for yourself. Out loud. Conspread is a gym for your thinking. Most apps give you answers. Conspread helps you build your own.

Open Conspread
How it works

Four steps to sharper thinking

  1. Say what you think firstWrite your view and how sure you are, from 1 to 10, before you see anyone else's.
  2. Meet the six lensesSix different ways of looking push back on your idea. They test it instead of agreeing with it.
  3. Say where you landWrite your view again. Did it change? How sure are you now?
  4. Watch your mind growA timeline shows how your thinking changes over time.
An example

"AI will replace most jobs in 10 years."

Before · sure: 7 out of 10

"Yes, most jobs will go."

A lens pushes back

Tech companies may gain while some workers lose. But new training has always come slowly after new technology. The direction may be right, but the 10 years may not.

After · sure: 4 out of 10

"Many jobs will change. 'Most' is too big a word."

Less certain, more precise. That is good thinking.

Who it's for

For questions you feel sure about

Conspread is for people who feel they were taught what to think, but not how to think. It is not for quick facts. The extra effort is on purpose.

  • Try it without signing in, or sign in with Google to save your thinking history.
  • Speak or type your question.
  • Save your thinking as PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text.
