Three Books

Three books, one journey. The first dreams of a new world. The second helps you meet yourself. The third is a workbook full of activities for children.

Through the Looking GlassA Journey of Perception and Understanding
Book 1 · 2023

Through the Looking Glass

This book shares the lessons Jitin learned and wanted to pass on. It dreams of a "better we because of a better me", instead of a "better me alone".

It is not a normal self-help book. It mixes knowing yourself with thinking about the future. It asks big questions and invites you to think beyond AI and to explore what humans can still become.

Meeting MyselfThe Tools You Already Carry
Book 2 · 2026

Meeting Myself

Your BFF for life: your mirror, your mentor, your true self, your friend and your guide. This book is for students, young people and grown-ups who feel confused, pressured or unsure about themselves.

It starts with one question: who do you talk to the most every day? The answer is you. Your inner voice talks to you all day. The book shows you how to listen to it wisely, so you can move from confusion and comparison to calm confidence.

Building TomorrowWorkbook · Volume 1
Book 3 · Workbook for ages 10+

Building Tomorrow

A 16-chapter workbook where children draw, write, role-play and think. There are no right or wrong answers. It is a mirror, not a test. Every chapter has a page for parents and teachers too.

It follows trusted ideas about how children grow, like the CASEL social and emotional learning framework. It has been tried in schools, and each chapter links to the UN Global Goals.

A note for grown-ups

Read together, grow together

Please hold space with love and curiosity. Listen, reflect and explore alongside the child. Every answer is a window into how a child thinks and feels. It is something to understand, not to correct.
